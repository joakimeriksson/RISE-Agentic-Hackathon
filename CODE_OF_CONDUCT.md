# Workshop code of conduct

Be respectful and patient. Welcome beginners, share explanations, rotate driver/reviewer roles and ask before changing someone else's work. Do not harass, discriminate, share credentials or pressure others to disclose personal data. Respect accessibility needs and requests to pause.

For a concern, contact the named event facilitator privately. The organizer must supply that contact before invitations; no fictional contact address is provided. Facilitators may pause work or remove a participant to protect others, and should handle reports confidentially within organizational policy.
